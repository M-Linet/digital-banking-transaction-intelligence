# Management recommendations

Each recommendation follows from a specific result (the query or notebook is named). All figures describe the fictional Tausi Digital Bank. Value estimates are fee revenue, not profit.

| # | Action | Evidence | Estimated value | Owner | Success measure (dashboard KPI) | First step |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Agent float programme for paydays and weekends**: float alerts for high-volume agents, same-day top-up from the 24th of each month and on Fridays, and super-agent liquidity support | q12, q19: float failures are 6% of agent withdrawals on paydays and weekends vs 1% otherwise. They are the largest single source of lost fees (KES 209k unrecovered over 18 months) and leave customers without cash when they most need it. | Most of KES 0.14M a year of lost fees, plus customer trust at agents | Head of Agency Banking | Agent float failure rate below 2% on payday days and weekends (*Failures by reason* filtered to Agent) | Rank agents by payday withdrawal volume and start alerts with the top 100 |
| 2 | **USSD evening capacity**: agree peak session capacity (18:00–21:00) with the network partner, and keep moving small transfers to the app | q9, q11: USSD has the highest operational failure rate (3.3%), rising to about 5% in the evening peak. Session drops are 64% of USSD operational failures. | Fewer lost transactions for the customers least likely to have the app | Head of Digital Channels + partner manager | USSD operational failure rate at 18:00–21:00 below 3% (*Reliability* page, hourly chart) | Share the hourly failure profile with the partner and agree a capacity test |
| 3 | **Refund the overcharges and add a daily fee reconciliation** that recomputes every fee from the tariff in force | q20: KES 96,444 charged against the tariff across 4,051 transactions in two defects (Feb 2026 and Mar–Apr 2026) | Avoids conduct and complaint risk. The control costs little because the SQL already exists. | Head of Finance Operations + Compliance | Zero tariff variances in the daily check (*Overcharge to refund* card = 0 for new periods) | Credit the 4,051 transactions and notify the affected customers |
| 4 | **App release controls**: staged roll-out (5% → 25% → 100%) with automatic rollback when timeouts exceed twice their normal level | q15: timeouts ran about seven times above normal for ten days after the March 2026 release, and the problem was never logged as an incident | Contains future release faults within hours instead of days | Head of Digital Engineering | Daily app timeout rate never above 2× its 30-day median for more than one day | Add the daily timeout rate to the release checklist |
| 5 | **Log every outage, using anomaly monitoring as a safety net** | Notebook 07: the anomaly model found all 11 logged incidents and three outages that were never logged | Complete incident history for root-cause analysis and partner SLAs | IT Service Management | Share of anomaly episodes with a matching incident record (target 100%) | Run the anomaly model weekly and review unmatched episodes |
| 6 | **Reactivate dormant customers and follow up on bad experiences**: a reactivation offer for the 5,591 dormant customers (Youth and Mass Retail first), and a proactive message or fee refund after 2+ operational failures in a month | q24, q28: 19% of customers are dormant. Within the same activity band, customers with 2+ failures were about twice as likely to be inactive the next month (4.4% vs 2.0% for 10+ attempts). | Each 1,000 reactivated Mass Retail or Youth customers adds about KES 0.85M of fees a year (KES 71 per active month) | Head of Customer Value Management | 90-day reactivation rate; next-month activity of customers who hit 2+ failures | Build the contact list from `v_customer_summary` and run the offer with a random hold-out |

## What I would *not* recommend yet
- **Reversing the wallet-transfer waiver to recover KES 62k a month.** It moved small transfers off USSD, the least reliable channel. Keep it, and measure the reliability benefit before deciding.
- **A churn or fraud model.** The simple rules here (dormancy, failures in the month, the anomaly episodes) already identify the actions. A model should have to beat them.
